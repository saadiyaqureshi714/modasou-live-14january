👗 The Struggles of Everyday Fashion

Growing up, my mother stitched all my clothes. They weren’t just perfectly tailored—they were long-lasting, vibrant, and never wore out easily. Each piece was crafted with care, ensuring that it remained a staple in my wardrobe for years.

Fast forward to my college days and beyond, I still cherished those clothes. But with the rise of online shopping, I started choosing ready-made attire for the sake of convenience. It seemed easier, faster—but was it really better?

🛍 Online shopping? Clothes often arrived ill-fitting, mismatched, or poor in quality.
🧵 Custom tailoring? So many unstitched saree blouses piled up in my wardrobe because I never found the time to get them stitched.
💰 Fast fashion? Everything was designed to wear out quickly, forcing us to buy more and waste more.

I searched for an online platform that could help me customize and tailor clothes effortlessly, just like the perfectly stitched outfits my mother made. While a few online customized clothing platforms existed, they came with their own set of frustrations:

🚫 Not user-friendly – Too many confusing tabs, and some didn’t even work.
⏳ Time-consuming – The entire process felt tedious rather than seamless.
🏠 Strangers entering my home? No thanks! I didn’t want to rely on someone visiting just to take measurements.
📦 Slow delivery – Waiting more than a month for a custom outfit wasn’t practical.

I realized that something was missing—a platform that blends the craftsmanship of custom tailoring with the ease and efficiency of online shopping.

That’s when MODASOU was born.

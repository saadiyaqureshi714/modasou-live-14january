Terms and Conditions

MODASOU is a design-led luxury fashion platform offering made-to-order and customized garments. Operated by an individual proprietor. Company incorporation in progress. Payments are processed under the proprietor account until formal incorporation is completed.
Article I

Business Structure

MODASOU is currently operated by an individual proprietor. The brand is in the process of formal company incorporation under applicable Indian laws. Until incorporation is completed, all transactions, invoices, and payments are processed under the proprietor's name.

Article II

Nature of Services

MODASOU provides design-led, made-to-order and customized fashion services, including garment design coordination, tailoring facilitation, and personalization support. All products are customized specifically for the customer based on selected design, measurements, and preferences.

Article III

Payments

All payments made on the MODASOU website are processed through authorized third-party payment gateways and credited to the proprietor account operating the MODASOU brand. By completing a transaction, the customer acknowledges and agrees to this arrangement during the pre-incorporation phase.

Article IV

Returns & Alterations Policy

As all garments are made-to-order and customized, returns and refunds are not permitted. However, MODASOU offers one complimentary alteration in cases where minor fit adjustments are required, subject to feasibility and review.

Article V

Invoices & Documentation

Invoices and payment receipts will be issued in the name of the individual proprietor operating under the MODASOU brand until formal incorporation is completed.

Article VI

Future Incorporation

Upon completion of company incorporation, MODASOU reserves the right to transfer all operations, payments, and contractual relationships to the incorporated entity without impacting existing customer orders or service commitments.

Article VII

Governing Law

These terms shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of India.

Article VIII

Privacy Policy – Data Handling

Customer data collected on the MODASOU platform is managed by the individual proprietor and will be handled with strict confidentiality. Customer data will not be shared with any third-party companies for marketing or resale purposes. Upon incorporation, data may be transferred to the registered entity while maintaining the same level of protection and privacy.

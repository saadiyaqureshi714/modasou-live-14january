Return & Refund Policy

At MODASOU, every garment is thoughtfully crafted and customized exclusively for you. Our designs are made-to-measure, based on your selected fabric, design preferences, and body measurements. Because of this personalized nature, our return and refund policy differs from ready-made apparel.

Returns & Refunds

  • All MODASOU garments are custom-made and personalized.
  • Returns, exchanges, or refunds are not applicable once an order has been confirmed and production has begun.
  • This policy applies to:
  • Custom measurements
  • Customized designs
  • Fabric selection and tailoring preferences

We encourage customers to carefully review measurements, design details, and order specifications before placing an order.

Complimentary Alteration Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us.

  • One (1) free alteration is provided by MODASOU for every custom order.
  • The free alteration covers fit-related adjustments only (e.g., minor looseness or tightness).
  • Alteration requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery.
  • The garment must be:
    • Unworn (except for trial)
    • Unwashed
    • In original condition

Alteration Logistics

  • Customers may be required to ship the garment back for alteration.
  • Return shipping costs may be borne by the customer unless otherwise specified.
  • MODASOU will handle the alteration and ship the garment back after completion.

What Alterations Do Not Cover

The complimentary alteration does not apply to:

  • Design changes after delivery
  • Change of fabric, color, or pattern
  • Significant resizing due to incorrect measurements provided by the customer
  • Damage caused after delivery (wear and tear, washing, mishandling)

Additional alterations beyond the complimentary one may be offered at an extra cost.

Measurement Responsibility

  • Accurate measurements are essential for a perfect fit.
  • MODASOU provides measurement guides and tutorials to support customers.
  • Final fit responsibility rests on the measurements shared by the customer.

Support & Assistance

If you have concerns about the fit or workmanship:

Contact us at shanthi@modasou.org within 7 days of receiving your order.

Our team will review your request and guide you through the alteration process.

Our Promise

MODASOU stands for craftsmanship, customization, and care. While custom-made garments cannot be returned or refunded, we are committed to ensuring your outfit fits beautifully through our complimentary alteration service.

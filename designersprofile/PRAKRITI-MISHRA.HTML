About the Designer

I am a fashion graduate with over 10 years of professional experience, specializing in ethnic and fusion wear. My expertise extends deeply into bridal couture, where refined craftsmanship, intricate detailing, and personalized design are at the heart of every creation.

I have completed specialized training in bridal wear from the London School of Trends and have been mentored by Manish Malhotra, an experience that has profoundly shaped my design sensibility and approach to luxury fashion.

Over the years, I have designed for thousands of satisfied clients. Witnessing their confidence and happiness is the greatest reward of my journey. Creating meaningful, beautifully crafted garments that leave a lasting impact continues to be my true source of fulfillment.
